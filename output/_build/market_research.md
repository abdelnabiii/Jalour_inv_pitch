- 2026 annual growth | New Cairo 10 to 15%, Mostakbal City 12 to 16% (estimate from a blog drawing on Aqarmap data, not an index) | 2026 | Sands of Wealth | https://sandsofwealth.com/blogs/news/cairo-price-forecasts
- Residential average, New Cairo | EGP 98,000/sqm (Q2 2025, as reported; seems high versus listings, verify) | Q2 2025 | search summary | https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/hardhats/cairos-rental-market-running-double-digit-increase-while-sales-stall/
- Read-across: the 2023 to 2024 surge came with devaluation. By 2025 to 2026 growth is 10 to 16% a year in nominal EGP, and secondary-market growth in Q1 2026 was only 3.1%. Compounded 10 to 16% for 3 to 4 years gives roughly 33% to 81%, so 40 to 60% is feasible only at about 12 to 13% a year, which is at the top of the evidence for secondary sales and contingent on EGP inflation (about 14.5%). In USD terms, office rents were only +2% y/y.

## 6. Risks

- Oversupply | Knight Frank: pipeline +82% of stock by 2029; analysts report luxury oversupply and unsold stock | 2026 | Arabian Business; Enterprise | URLs in sections 1 and 4
- Cooling demand | top 10 developers volumes -15% (Q1) and -5% (H1) y/y; investor-buyer share down to under 20% | 2026 | Daily News Egypt | https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/?p=849148
- Delivery delays | units sold in 2022 to 2023 approaching handover; some handovers delayed 2 to 3 years; finishing costs more than doubled since 2022 | 2026 | Enterprise (search summary) | https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/hardhats/egypts-real-estate-market-has-been-cooling-since-even-before-the-war-on-iran/
- State relief | land instalment interest cut to 12 to 15% for one year to help delivery | 2026 | Enterprise (search summary) | https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/?p=668099
- Payment plans | typically 8 to 12 years, up to 14 years at some projects; down payments 1.5% to 10% | 2026 | Bayut / Property Finder listings | https://www.bayut.eg/en/property/details-503888574.html
- Off-plan rules | PM Decree 2184 of 2022 (effective 17 Jun 2022): developers on state land must open a separate project bank account, follow approved timelines, and may not announce sales before the Ministerial decisions approving the general plan and the detailed phase plan | 2022, in force | Invest-Gate / Mondaq | https://invest-gate.me/news/prime-minister-sets-regulations-for-sale-of-real-estate-development-units/ ; https://www.mondaq.com/real-estate/1700074/overview-on-real-estate-development-in-egypt
- Mandatory escrow | only a parliamentary proposal (MP Ahmed Fayed) for per-project escrow accounts with staged release; not law as of the source | 2026 | Daily News Egypt | https://www.dailynewsegypt.com/?p=851498
- Resale and assignment | developers' contracts commonly let them assign rights to mortgage finance firms with notification to the buyer (Civil Code Art. 305 on assignment effect); buyer-side resale of contracts is common but "stagnant resale" reported. Transfer fees in new cities reportedly cut to 2 to 3% from 10% (search summary, source unverified) | 2026 | Enterprise; Housing Ministry blog | https://enterpriseam.com/egypt/hardhats/real-estate-buyers-are-reselling-their-contracts-and-analysts-disagree-on-why/ ; https://blogs.realestate.gov.eg/news/cairo-new-capital-developers-association-praises-housing-ministers-new-decisions/
- Specific FRA rule on developer consent to off-plan resale | not found. Developer approval and fees are contractual, so check each developer's assignment policy and the MIDAR / NUCA land-sale terms.
